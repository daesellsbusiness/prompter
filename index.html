Teleprompter

Paste your script, then open the camera. The words scroll at the top of the screen, right under the camera, so you look like you're talking to the viewer.

  • Hold the phone at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  • Face a window for the best light.
  • Tap the words to pause or resume. Drag them to scroll by hand.
  • It doesn't have to be word for word. Natural beats perfect.
