Teleprompter

Paste your script, then open the camera. The script floats near the top, right under the camera, so you look like you're talking to the viewer. Read the bright lines in the middle of the box.

  • Hold the phone at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  • Face a window for the best light.
  • Tap the words to pause or resume. Drag them to scroll by hand.
  • Drag the little bar under the script to move the box. Speed changes how fast it scrolls.
  • It doesn't have to be word for word. Natural beats perfect.
